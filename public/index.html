Our Story

We Invite You to Visit
The Taj Multicuisine Restaurant

Perched gracefully on the 2nd Floor above HDFC Bank on Lower Dakbunglow Road, The Taj Multicuisine Restaurant brings a refined dining experience to the heart of Hajipur. Created for celebratory family dinners, reunion lunches, and royal feasts, our hall is designed to make every meal feel memorable.

Freshly Ground Masalas & Charcoal Tandoor

Our chefs craft every masala from scratch, slow-cooking curries to silky perfection and smoking fresh kebabs over glowing charcoal embers.

Pure Veg & Non-Veg Segregation

We maintain strict hygiene protocols and dedicated preparation stations for pure vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes.

Private Banquets & Family Celebrations

Air-conditioned celebration suites accommodating up to 70 guests for birthdays, anniversaries, and corporate gatherings.
