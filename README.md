# The Taj Multicuisine Restaurant

Official website for **The Taj Multicuisine Restaurant**, Hajipur, Bihar.

Located on the 2nd Floor, Above HDFC Bank, Lower Dakbunglow Road, Hajipur, Bihar 844101.

---

## Overview

This website features:
- **Royal Mughlai & Awadhi Dum Biryani**, Sizzling Tandoor Grills, and North Indian Curries.
- **Interactive Menu Explorer** with detailed popups and calorie/prep-time breakdowns.
- **Table Reservation & Banquet Booking** for family celebrations up to 70 guests.
- **Responsive Design** powered by Bootstrap 5, Swiper, AOS scroll animations, and Magnific Popup.
- **Direct Contact & Directions** to Lower Dakbunglow Road, Hajipur.
